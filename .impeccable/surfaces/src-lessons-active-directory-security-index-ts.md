## Direction contract

OWN-WORLD: Diazo whiteprint sheets — cool lilac-grey paper, violet line ink, drafting-red pencil for routes and revisions, checker's yellow highlighter for what is verified. Grid bubbles, door swings, hatched walls, hexagon keynote tags, revision clouds, title blocks, transmittal ticks. Plex Sans Condensed lettering, Plex Mono for schedules and event IDs.

STORY: Students see the estate as a drawing, watch configuration (not exploits) open a path, follow svc_backup storey by storey to DCSync, then learn why one revision of KRBTGT is not enough and what verifying replication means.
