# Instructor-led presentation
Mode: Read, experienced together in a classroom.
Scope: Present route and authored presentation plans. Keep existing lesson palettes, IBM Plex family, Study and Workshop.
Audience: an 85-inch 16:9 display viewed from up to five metres.
Direction: a large editorial teaching canvas. Session route map, chapter outlines, one teaching point per slide; evidence excerpts, process diagrams, comparisons and discussion pauses. No article blocks or scrolling panes.
First viewport: an expansive lesson title, central teaching question and Observe → Reason → Decide chain, with lesson color inherited. A full-screen control stays discoverable.
Signature interaction: presenter-controlled reveals; each step adds one evidence row, comparison or process node. Arrows/Space advance; A reveals all. Motion traces process connectors before revealing the next explanation; evidence and annotations build in sequence. M toggles motion, while device reduced motion always takes precedence. Every effect is finite and interruptible; prior points remain still.
Type contract: 1920×1080 reference canvas, 84px headings,48px bullets,38px code,36px annotations,26px running metadata. Scale the complete frame uniformly; never reduce individual text to fit. Full source remains linked.
Acceptance: all ten authored plans, valid source links, no clipping or internal slide scrolling, keyboard/overview navigation, source progress/resume, reduced motion and production build. Verify rendered desktop and phone sizes; physical room check remains a user environment check.

Motion thesis: the focal moment is an evidence chain gaining its next link. Continuity uses short directional slide transitions; comparisons reveal the new side, evidence reveals the excerpt before its annotation, and section outlines enter as a bounded list. Feedback is the current flow node and visible motion state. Budget: no loops or dependencies; at most five build items, capped180ms stagger, routine effects260–360ms and cover sequence660ms. Stable layout and source links retain the reading contract.
