## Direction contract

THESIS: A hunt is a specialist examining a sheet of identical stamps for one listed variety. Naming the variety, its position and the lamp it shows under is what makes both "found" and "examined, not present" worth recording. Refuses the dark SIEM dashboard with radar sweeps and the hunter's crosshair.

OWN-WORLD: Warm black stockbook page with faint glassine strips; specimens are paper-white faces with perforation teeth, engraved guilloche and a numeral-09 definitive. Named stamp inks: ultramarine (theory), carmine (adversary, practical), bistre (hypothesis), emerald (confirmed), lilac (uncertain), cyan (response). Sheet-margin language: plate number, colour-check ink dots, row/column positions. Plex Serif engraved capitals for the display titles (title slide, page hero); slide and section heads stay sentence-case Plex Serif because an ESL room reads them as sentences; Plex Sans Condensed for margin inscriptions and catalogue lines, Plex Mono only for catalogue numbers, positions and counts. The one glow is UV phosphor, pale yellow-green, and only under the lamp.

STORY: The student sees that a hypothesis names what to look for, where, and under which light before looking; that the rare unit lives in the long tail; that every examined position is annotated whether or not it held anything; and that a confirmed find becomes a catalogue listing (a rule) so nobody hunts it twice.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title slide. Left ~55%: a pane of twenty numeral-09 stamps (5x4) in ultramarine, perforated, one inverted-centre variety at the stack-counting slide's position (R2/2), the same position the backdrop lamp reaches on that slide; a lamp passes position by position leaving pencil ticks, stops on the variety and pins its catalogue annotation. Right: "Session 9" in Plex Serif at display scale, the session title as the stamp's engraved label, the lead below, and the overline set beneath it as the issue's catalogue line. Behind everything the stockbook backdrop: a dark sheet under a UV lamp that pans so the current slide's sheet position sits under the lamp. Study/overview hero: title, lead and actions left, a smaller pane right with the same variety.
